A quiet minute with your camera

Breathe Together watches your breathing over your webcam and adjusts a gentle pacing guide to match it. It only uses the camera after you say so, and only for as long as you let it.

  • What the camera is for: estimating your breathing rhythm so the pacing guide can keep pace with you.
  • Where it is processed: frames travel to the small local program on this same computer, where they are analyzed and then dropped — never saved as video. They never leave this device.
  • What the vendor sees: the measurement engine checks your API key and usage with the vendor's servers when a session starts. No frames, no breathing numbers, and no personal details are sent.
  • What is saved: nothing by default. No video is stored or uploaded.
  • How to stop: the Stop button is always visible while the camera is on. It releases the camera immediately.
  • Each visit: consent is asked again every time you load the page.
Why camera?

The pacing guide works best when it can see your breathing rhythm. The camera picture is analyzed on this computer to estimate that rhythm — frames are used for the estimate and then discarded; they are never recorded, saved, or uploaded. The measurement software may contact its maker (Presage) to verify your API key and plan usage when a session starts; that contact carries the key and a device check only — never video or measurements. Its optional usage-statistics reporting is switched off in this app. Full details are in the project's consent notes (`docs/CONSENT_AND_DATA.md`). If you would rather not use the camera at all, the breathing guide can run on its own — decline the camera permission and choose guide-only mode.

After you click, your browser will ask for camera permission. You can decline — nothing starts without it.
